Privacy Policy — Cosy Home UK

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

Cosy Home UK (cosyhomeuk.co.uk) is a Pinterest curation account sharing UK home decor and interiors ideas. You can reach us via our Pinterest profile at pinterest.com/cosyhomeuk.

What data we collect

None, from visitors. This site is a static page. We do not run analytics, set cookies, or collect, store, or process personal data from anyone who visits it.

Our Pinterest app integration acts solely on our own Pinterest account (creating boards and pins). It does not access, collect, or store data about other Pinterest users.

Affiliate links

Some pins on our Pinterest account contain affiliate links. If you buy through one, the merchant may pay us a commission at no extra cost to you. Affiliate pins are labelled #ad. Purchases happen on the merchant's own website, which has its own privacy policy — we never see your payment or order details.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log standard server data (such as IP addresses) when you visit; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page.
