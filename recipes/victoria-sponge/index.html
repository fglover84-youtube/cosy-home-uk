Victoria sponge

Serves 825 min prep + 22 min baketwo 20cm tins

The classic equal-weights sponge: weigh your eggs in their shells, then match the butter, sugar and flour to them. It has never once let us down.

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs (~200g in shell)
  • 200g soft butter + extra for tins
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 200g self-raising flour, sifted
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 4 tbsp raspberry or strawberry jam
  • 150ml double cream, softly whipped
  • Icing sugar, to dust

Method

  1. Heat oven to 180°C fan. Butter and line two 20cm sandwich tins.
  2. Beat butter and sugar until pale and fluffy — 3–4 minutes, longer than you think.
  3. Add eggs one at a time with a spoonful of flour each (stops curdling), then the vanilla. Fold in the rest of the flour and baking powder gently.
  4. Divide between tins, level, bake 20–22 minutes until golden and springing back.
  5. Cool in tins 5 minutes, then onto a rack. When cold, sandwich with jam then cream, and dust the top with icing sugar.
